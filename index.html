How to Play Sudoku

Master the rules, controls, and strategies of Sudoku Arena.

1

The Core Rule

Every row, every column, and every 3×3 sub-box must contain the digits 1 through 9 without repetition.

2

Drag & Drop Number Pad

Press and drag any number from the bottom pad directly onto an empty cell. Numbers automatically disappear from the pad when all 9 occurrences are correctly placed!

3

Pencil Notes Mode

Toggle Notes (N) to place small candidate numbers in empty cells. When you place a final number, notes in peer cells are pruned automatically.

4

Mistakes & Hints

You are allowed up to 3 mistakes per game. If you get stuck, click Hint (H) to reveal a correct number for an unsolved cell.

5

Solving Score

Scores are calculated from your chosen difficulty, completion time, mistake count, and hints used. Faster times and fewer hints yield higher scores!
